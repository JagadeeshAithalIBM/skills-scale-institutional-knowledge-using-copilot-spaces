# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define QA strategy, manage test planning, and validate release readiness. They ensure that deliverables meet quality standards and that defects are managed systematically throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and testing approach for each release
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Coordinate testing activities across phases (unit, integration, system, UAT)
- Manage defect reporting, tracking, and resolution
- Validate release readiness and provide sign-off
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Lead post-release monitoring and issue resolution

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Reviews test results, communicates defect findings, collaborates on root cause analysis and fixes
- **With Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria, validates feature quality before release
- **With Project Managers**: Reports quality metrics and risks, ensures QA activities are scheduled appropriately
- **With Release Manager**: Provides quality assessment for go/no-go decisions
- **With Execution Team**: Coordinates testing schedules and resource allocation

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet acceptance criteria
- Minimize defects in production releases
- Provide confidence in release readiness
- Reduce rework and cycle time

### Typical Communication
- QA status reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Defect and test case documentation
- Release readiness assessments and sign-off
- Quality reviews and planning sessions

---

## Risk & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Risk & Compliance Officers identify, assess, and mitigate risks throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure that projects comply with organizational policies, regulatory requirements, and industry standards.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and update the risk register
- Facilitate risk identification workshops with stakeholders
- Assess risk probability and impact, prioritize mitigation efforts
- Develop and track risk mitigation strategies and contingency plans
- Monitor compliance with organizational policies and regulatory requirements
- Conduct compliance audits and validations
- Escalate high-priority risks and compliance issues to project leadership
- Document lessons learned and compliance artifacts

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Collaborates on risk planning, reports risks requiring escalation
- **With Developers**: Discusses technical risks and security concerns
- **With Stakeholders**: Communicates regulatory requirements and compliance constraints
- **With Technical Leads**: Validates technical compliance and risk mitigation approaches
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Coordinates on quality and compliance validation

### Goals
- Proactively identify and mitigate project risks
- Ensure compliance with all applicable regulations and policies
- Minimize impact of unforeseen issues
- Build organizational risk awareness and resilience

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk dashboards
- Compliance checklists and audit reports
- Risk mitigation plans and escalation notices
- Regulatory requirement summaries

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical vision and guidance, validate design decisions, and ensure that solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards. They bridge business requirements and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design principles
- Review and validate technical design documents and proposals
- Assess technical feasibility of requirements and features
- Advise on technology stack selection and trade-offs
- Ensure adherence to architectural standards and best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Support capacity planning and scalability assessments
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and best practices

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides architectural guidance, reviews technical designs, mentors on patterns
- **With Product Managers**: Discusses technical feasibility and trade-offs for feature requests
- **With Project Managers**: Communicates technical risks and architectural dependencies
- **With Technical Leads**: Collaborates on design decisions and technical strategy
- **With Release Manager**: Provides guidance on deployment architecture and infrastructure considerations

### Goals
- Ensure technically sound and scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Align technical decisions with business objectives
- Enable team technical growth and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Architectural design documents and decision logs
- Technical feasibility assessments
- Code and design reviews
- Architecture discussions and technical forums

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons act as the bridge between the project team and business stakeholders. They manage expectations, collect feedback, and ensure stakeholder needs are understood and addressed throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain active communication with business stakeholders
- Gather and document stakeholder requirements and feedback
- Manage stakeholder expectations and concerns
- Conduct status updates and demo sessions for stakeholders
- Escalate stakeholder issues and blockers to project leadership
- Ensure stakeholder alignment on project scope, schedule, and risks
- Coordinate stakeholder reviews and approvals
- Document stakeholder feedback and incorporate into planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Channels stakeholder feedback into product backlog priorities
- **With Project Managers**: Communicates stakeholder concerns and expectations for project planning
- **With Developers**: Translates stakeholder feedback into clearer requirements and acceptance criteria
- **With Executive Sponsor**: Reports stakeholder sentiment and escalates critical issues
- **With Quality Assurance Lead**: Coordinates stakeholder acceptance testing

### Goals
- Maintain stakeholder satisfaction and engagement
- Prevent misalignment on project scope and deliverables
- Enable timely stakeholder feedback and decision-making
- Build trust and transparency with business partners

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder status reports and dashboards
- Meeting invitations and demo sessions
- Feedback summaries and requirement clarifications
- Escalation notices and issue resolutions

---

## Knowledge & Documentation Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge & Documentation Managers ensure that project processes, decisions, and lessons learned are systematically captured, organized, and accessible. They enable knowledge sharing across teams and support organizational learning.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project documentation (plans, designs, decisions, procedures)
- Develop and update process documentation and guidelines
- Capture and organize meeting notes and decision logs
- Facilitate knowledge transfer sessions and training activities
- Maintain centralized knowledge repositories and wikis
- Coordinate documentation reviews and updates
- Support onboarding of new team members through documentation
- Conduct retrospectives and capture lessons learned

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With All Roles**: Collects information and documentation from each team member
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinates on project documentation and process updates
- **With Developers**: Captures technical decisions and implementation details
- **With Product Managers**: Documents product requirements and feature specifications
- **With Process Leads**: Ensures consistency across process documentation

### Goals
- Build a comprehensive institutional knowledge base
- Reduce onboarding time and ramp-up for new team members
- Preserve organizational learning and best practices
- Enable consistency and repeatability across projects

### Typical Communication
- Documentation repositories and wikis
- Process guides and procedure manuals
- Training materials and onboarding resources
- Retrospective reports and lessons learned summaries
